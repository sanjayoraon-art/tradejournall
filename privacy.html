Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

At TradeJournall (accessible from https://tradejournall.com), the privacy and security of our users are of paramount importance. This Privacy Policy document describes the types of information collected and how it is protected.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect essential information required to operate our trading journal services smoothly:

  • Account Information: When you sign in, we process your email and profile authentication via secure Google Firebase Auth.
  • Trade Log Entries: Trade details manually entered by you (such as ticker symbol, buy/sell prices, quantity, strategies, and optional chart screenshots).
  • Technical Analytics: Anonymized performance metrics and browser crash telemetry to improve application uptime and response speed.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • To provide, operate, and maintain your personal trading journal and metrics dashboard.
  • To calculate performance KPIs such as Win Rate, Profit Factor, Risk-to-Reward ratio, and Drawdown.
  • To provide AI trading coaching insights based solely on your anonymized trade log context.
  • To protect against unauthorized access and maintain cloud synchronization.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your trade data is encrypted and securely stored using Google Cloud Firestore infrastructure. We never sell, lease, or rent your personal trade records to third parties, brokers, hedge funds, or advertisers.

4. Third-Party Integrations & Social Sharing

When you share blog guides or educational content to social media platforms (such as Pinterest, Twitter/X, or Facebook), those platforms operate under their respective privacy policies. We do not transmit your private trading logs to external social channels.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

We use your device's secure browser local storage to maintain session preferences (such as Dark/Light theme mode, timeframe filters, and preferred base currency: USD, INR, EUR, etc.).

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your stored data, please contact us at:
